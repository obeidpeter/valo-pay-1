# First-time-user research kit

Status: **prepared; human usability acceptance not yet tested**. This protocol contains no participants, results, quotations or measured success/ease scores. Keep actual human evidence separate from the engineering journey results in this programme. The [source notes](sources.md) define the controlling framework and product boundaries.

## Purpose and study design

Evaluate whether a first-time user can explain where they are, what the page is for, what their actual role permits, what to do next, whether work was saved/submitted and how to recover or get help. Unfamiliarity with Valo Pay does not mean a participant is qualified or authorised to make every financial decision.

Recruit independently from each materially different user group below. Begin with a small formative round planned by the research owner, then size later benchmark rounds for the precision needed. Do not claim a small convenience sample represents all lenders or SMEs. Record the invited, eligible, consented, completed and withdrawn counts for each cohort, including any missing sessions. People who designed this release can preflight the study but are not independent first-time participants.

These cohorts do not assign roles or create Credit analyst, Cash operator or Payroll checker capabilities. Read the current effective role/action matrix and purpose scope before the session. Some real staff routes differ from sandbox personas; for example, the baseline Credit Desk is a sandbox-persona workflow. Mark an unavailable staff, invitation, MFA, public bank hand-off or applicant route **setup blocked**, then assess its truthful blocked explanation separately. Switching personas demonstrates a control; it is not evidence of genuine independent-person approval.

Screen for task/domain experience, previous Valo Pay exposure, usual device/input, connection constraints and optional accessibility requirements. Collect only what affects study interpretation. Recruit keyboard and assistive-technology users for those conditions; do not infer their experience from automated tests or disability assumptions.

## Preparation and privacy

1. Obtain the research owner's named approval for recruitment, consent materials, collection, access, retention and deletion before collecting participant data. No new analytics, external recording service or messaging is authorised by this kit. Default to minimal manual notes. Recording requires separate, optional consent and an approved purpose.
2. Use a controlled synthetic environment with live financial actions and provider delivery disabled. Do not run mutation tasks on the shared deployed app or a real lender. Use a fresh participant fixture with the same lender/entity, fixed clock, source ages, record totals and role scope for comparable attempts.
3. Record the exact build/commit, environment, task variant, browser version, viewport, device type, theme, input method, assistive technology, zoom, connection condition and baseline/candidate phase. Label emulation and physical-device work separately.
4. Predeclare the expected authoritative result and safe stop for every card. Prepare one ordinary and one failure/recovery variant where applicable, including wrong-role, stale-data and uncertain-outcome states. Do not adjust the definition of success after seeing the attempt.
5. Preflight permissions and fixtures with a nonparticipant account. An unusable setup is not a participant failure. Do not rescue a blocked fixture by giving the participant Admin or broader grants.
6. For maker/checker tasks, prepare two independently identified authorised study accounts where the environment supports them. Otherwise test the visible refusal and pending-review explanation and record genuine independent-person completion as unavailable.
7. Do not collect passwords, MFA codes, identity documents, real bank records, payroll details, raw credit features or live application references. Use random participant and fixture codes. Keep raw research data and screen captures outside this public repository in the approved private location.
8. Present tasks in counterbalanced order where practical, while preserving necessary dependencies. Reset synthetic fixtures rather than relying on earlier participants' mutations. Use equivalent A/B records if comparing builds; report prior exposure and learning effects.

## Moderator script

Opening: “We are studying the product, not judging you. Use only the sample records provided. Work as you normally would. You can stop or take a break. Some actions may require another person's authority; deciding not to proceed can be the correct result.”

Obtain participation consent before observation. Explain what is recorded and how it will be used. Participation and recording choices must not affect work access or employment evaluation.

For orientation, open the appropriate starting screen and say: “Tell me what this workspace is for, whose work you are looking at, and what would be a sensible first action for you.” Start timing when the page is available. Stop at a complete answer or the one-minute target, then continue observing without rewriting the first result.

For each task, read only its participant prompt below. Do not reveal the destination, button label or expected path. Confirm they understand the goal without supplying interface instructions. Begin timing when they start. Use think-aloud for formative diagnosis, or normal silent work followed by retrospective questions for benchmark timing; record the method and do not pool those times.

If asked for help, first say: “What would you normally try next?” Record the request. Once a navigation hint, button name, corrective instruction or task-solving demonstration is supplied, stop the unassisted attempt and record the outcome before any coaching. A coached continuation is a separate attempt linked to the original, never a reclassified unassisted success. Record neutral domain clarification separately.

Use a predeclared session limit. Suggested starting limits are eight minutes for a routine task and twelve minutes for a paired review; pilot and freeze them before benchmarking. A timeout is incomplete, not success. Pause immediately for real data, an external effect, distress or a critical safety misunderstanding. Record the misunderstanding before coaching, without permitting harm.

After each attempt ask the same custom question: “Overall, how easy or difficult was this task?” Use 1 = very difficult, 2 = difficult, 3 = neither easy nor difficult, 4 = easy, 5 = very easy. Allow no answer. This is the brief's proposed five-point task-ease measure, not a validated SEQ or SUS instrument. Ask it for failed attempts too, before a demonstration influences the answer.

Finish with: “What did you expect to happen?”, “What told you your work was saved or submitted?” and “What would you do next?” Record genuine comments without adding implied agreement. Offer a debrief after measurements and explain any misleading financial interpretation encountered.

## Neutral task cards and facilitator outcomes

Give participants only the prompt column. Routes, roles and expected results are facilitator notes. Use the prepared sample identifiers in place of bracketed text.

| --- | --- | --- |
| U01 / every role | “You are starting work for [organisation]. Find the work available to you. Explain where you are, whether these are real customer records and which task you would start. Then return after the provided session interruption.” | Public entry plus synthetic role fixture. Correct organisation, role and environment; reaches a sensible permitted task. Wrong-account, expired invitation and missing-auth variants must explain the real recovery path. Do not collect credentials. Unavailable provider authentication is a setup limitation. |
| U02 / Operations import | “Add the supplied sample payment file. Some entries may need attention. Work out what can be accepted, correct what is needed and tell us what has actually been added.” | Existing supported observations/import format and the actual example file; at least one invalid row. The participant distinguishes saved draft, checked preview and committed batch, identifies the field/row error, and commits once. Verify server counts and original operation identity. Lost-answer variant recovers that request, without a second batch. |
| U02 / Finance match | “The sample payment may belong to [repayment]. Decide what should happen to it, explain the evidence and find any unresolved work left over.” | Correct customer, reference, currency, amount, remaining obligation and proposal evidence; Finance reviews or safely rejects under current authority. A stale proposal forces refreshed review. No unsupported allocation or artificial success from clicking a button. |
| U03 / Operations maker | “A colleague must take over [issue]. Leave enough information for them to continue, explain what remains unresolved and prepare the day's work for review.” | Existing case assignment/handover and close preparation. Verify saved owner/history/note and dated close. Distinguish issue resolution from payment allocation/refund and distinguish prepared close from independent approval. |
| U03 / Finance reviewer | “Review the work prepared by your colleague for [date]. Decide whether it is ready to approve, explain any problem and obtain the evidence you are allowed to use.” | Distinct reviewer and current close version. Author cannot self-approve; changed evidence invalidates review. Correct block/escalation counts as success only in a predeclared blocked variant. Genuine separate-person approval requires two people and supported identity. |
| U04 / permitted reader | “Find the evidence for [customer or close]. Start the appropriate file request. The connection will be interrupted; find out what happened and retrieve the correct file if available.” | Correct lender/object/format, existing export job or original uncertain request. Verify saved job state and download identity using authorised synthetic evidence. Loss of permission means safe refusal. No fresh request to hide unknown status and no claim that download can be recalled. |
| U05 / Admin or Operations maker | “Prepare a request for [customer] to pay the supplied repayment. Explain who would receive the money, the amount and any fee, and what the current result means.” | Existing eligible sample intent and one-time purpose. Compare obligation and beneficiary; preserve existing collection/debit race guards. The simulator does not calculate or charge payment fees and offers no live fee quote; the correct explanation must not infer free live payments. Synthetic authorisation and browser return cannot be described as live bank approval or receipt. |
| U05 / payer interpretation | “You have returned after trying to pay this bill. Tell us what happened and what you would do next.” | Show prepared pending, unknown and confirmed-sample variants separately. Unknown does not trigger a fresh payment; participant finds status/recovery. Correctly names merchant/amount and distinguishes receipt from settlement where displayed. Public real bank authorisation is unavailable unless separately commissioned. |
| U06 / credit maker | “Prepare the supplied application for a qualified colleague to review. Work out what information is missing and explain what the result can and cannot tell them.” | Separate current read/assessment grants, one thin-file sample and one sufficient example. Missing evidence is unavailable, never zero risk; assessment/recommendation is not approval or disbursement. Verify saved version and review status; no new privilege from selecting a goal. |
| U06 / credit reviewer | “Review the assessment your colleague prepared for [application]. Decide what the evidence supports and leave an explanation the applicant could understand.” | Independent permitted reviewer; current evidence/model/policy version and applicant explanation. | U07 / Cash reader | “Work out the cash position of [business] and decide how reliable it is for today's planning. Find the related accounting work and tell us whether it is already in the books.” | Timestamped source balances including one stale/unavailable account, separate commitments/forecast and an accounting draft. Participant describes coverage/age and draft versus posted state. Refresh, back and deep-link return preserve the chosen section. |
| U08 / accounting maker and reviewer | “Prepare the sample receipt for the accounting team, then review the version prepared by a colleague. Explain what happens after the file is obtained.” | Current accounting-purpose grant, mapping and period evidence. Separate maker and Finance reviewer; changed data blocks old approval. | U08 / Finance VAT review | “Prepare the evidence for [tax period]. Find any gaps that need review and explain what the resulting file means.” | Current purpose, period and approved invoice/receipt evidence. Saved schedule may include unresolved gaps; changed evidence or permission restricts reuse. Export is neither filed tax nor remittance; no tax judgement is inferred from novice navigation. |
| U08 / payroll maker | “Prepare the supplied approved salary run for a finance colleague. Work out whether the funding evidence is sufficient and explain the next step.” | Approved net-pay source; current purpose; totals, beneficiaries, date, commitments, fees and source age. | U08 / payroll checker | “Review your colleague's salary preparation and decide whether it can be handed to the bank process. Afterwards, explain which items have actually been paid.” | Different Finance actor; frozen plan/version and supported file export. Changed funding/beneficiary invalidates approval. Exported is unpaid; unknown item remains held and excluded from a fresh export. Verify per-item evidence, never a single misleading Complete label. |
| U09 / purpose, refusal and revocation | “Explain what access [organisation] is asking for and whether it lets them take a payment. Decide whether to allow the optional purpose. Later, find out how to stop further use and what happens to earlier records.” | Separate account-read, assessment and payment authority. Do not preselect optional consent. Use actual grant/revoke authority; read-only participant explains escalation. Revoking account-read access does not stop a recurring debit mandate; that requires its separate supported mandate route. Revocation blocks new dependent work and does not falsely promise erasure or cancellation of submitted work. No invented public consent route. |
| U10 / every role | “You cannot finish the supplied task after the interruption. Find out what is saved or still being checked, choose a safe next step and find help relevant to your problem. Then return to your earlier work.” | Prepared missing-permission, no-records, filtered-empty, slow-read, validation or unknown-operation variant. Verify current server state before retry, role-scoped help/destination, retained safe filters and draft context. A sensitive unsaved browser draft need not survive reload; the product must explain the real limit without unsafe local storage. |

U02, U03, U05, U06 and U08 contain distinct tasks. Report their variants separately rather than making one combined completion score. A participant should receive only tasks relevant to their role and experience; nobody must perform the whole portfolio.

## Measures and decision rules

These are **initial project targets from the brief**, not established results or Nielsen-prescribed thresholds. Freeze the tasks, outcome rules and thresholds before a benchmark round. Use formative observations to improve the product without pretending they form a statistically conclusive release certificate.

| Measure | Recording and calculation | Initial target / handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orientation | Time to correct workspace, organisation/environment and sensible permitted first action; record each component and assistance | Within 60 seconds without coaching. A confident wrong answer is a critical misunderstanding, not success. |
| Routine task completion | Correct unassisted completed attempts / all eligible first unassisted attempts, separately by role, task and variant | At least 90%. Timeouts, wrong outcomes and assisted attempts are not numerator successes. Report raw n/N. Report setup-blocked, withdrawal and missing observations separately; do not quietly discard task-caused failures. |
| Correct safety stop | In predeclared blocked variants, participant identifies the unmet condition and the supported next action | Report separately from routine execution. It can be the intended correct outcome, but must not inflate the routine task-completion numerator. |
| Critical comprehension | Per applicable distinction: sample/live, pending/confirmed, assessment/decision, draft/posted, exported/executed; record correct, incorrect or not assessed | Every tested participant answers correctly where relevant. Report the exact n/N per distinction and role. Any incorrect critical interpretation requires a tracked finding and retest; averages cannot hide it. |
| Safe recovery | Record saved/request identity before interruption and observed authoritative state after; check duplicate effects and lost saved work | No silent loss of saved work and no duplicate consequential submission in tested scenarios. Unknown remains unknown until evidence resolves it. Report each tested condition and scope. |
| Perceived ease | The same five-point custom question after every attempt, including failures; calculate mean plus distribution and respondent count per role/task | Average at least 4/5. Keep missing responses visible and do not replace comprehension or completion with confidence/ease. |
| Diagnostic effort | Elapsed time, observed system wait, wrong turns, hints and recovery time | No threshold set by this brief. Record to explain difficulty; do not label synthetic browser timing human performance. |

Record an outcome as `correct_unassisted`, `correct_assisted`, `correct_safe_stop`, `incorrect`, `incomplete`, `setup_blocked` or `withdrew`. Assisted continuations have a linked new row. Critical misunderstandings remain findings even if later coached successfully. Distinguish system wait from active effort when observable; do not fabricate a split.

## Using the empty CSV

[measurement-template.csv](measurement-template.csv) contains a header only. No blank cell means zero, no result means pass, and no planned participant implies recruitment. Make an authorised private copy for actual sessions; do not commit filled participant records to this repository.

Use one row per participant/task/variant/attempt. Use separate rows for orientation and each coached continuation. Allowed `phase` values are baseline, candidate or retest; `attempt_type` is unassisted or coached; `study_method` is formative_think_aloud or timed_retrospective. `physical_or_emulated` must state which was used. `human_evidence_type` is observed, self_report or combined; do not place automation results in this template.

For each comprehension field use correct, incorrect or not_assessed with a short permitted observation reference. Record times only when measured. `server_outcome_reference` is a minimal synthetic record/job code, never a payload or credential. `assistance_detail` records the first hint and its timing. `critical_misunderstanding` is yes/no only after assessment, otherwise leave blank. Use `finding_id` to connect the issue to the usability register and `retest_of_attempt_id` to link the later independent attempt. Retests do not erase first-attempt failures.

Aggregate only comparable role/task/variant/build conditions. Show raw counts, setup failures, missing observations, assistance and limitations beside every rate or average. Report independent-human, keyboard, screen-reader, physical-device and slow-network coverage separately. If those sessions have not run, say **not yet tested**.

## Research completion and release hand-off

The researcher reviews recorded attempts against predeclared outcomes, then classifies findings using all ten heuristics. Financial misunderstanding, unsafe submission, lost saved work and blocked routine tasks take priority over visual polish. A material finding must retain its observed step, evidence, consequence, proposed fix and independent retest result.

Before claiming human acceptance, publish an anonymised aggregate report with exact build, cohort/task sample sizes, recruitment limits, failures, assistance, applicable critical comprehension, task-ease distribution and open dependencies. Do not publish raw financial or identifiable participant material. Obtain the agreed research review rather than using the implementer's role-play as acceptance.

This kit itself grants no recruitment, notification, access, financial, merge or deployment authority. No real participant session has been conducted for this programme at preparation time.
