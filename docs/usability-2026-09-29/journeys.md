## Review findings closed by the integrated help follow-up

The first integration mapped Mandates and Policies & templates to the account-read/credit/cash permission guide. That could send someone seeking to stop recurring debits to the wrong revocation workflow. Dedicated `mandates` and `policies` guides now distinguish routes, permitted roles and actual effects. Their checks cover navigation from the real pages, no write caused by reading help, sandbox-only mandate changes, and separate policy application requirements. The root integrates the matching route IDs.

The navigation review also found that Find a page intercepted Escape with an already-empty filter, preventing the mobile drawer’s usual Escape-to-close behaviour. The root owns that fix and its verification.

## Additional engineering execution in this work

`pnpm exec vitest run --config vitest.config.ts tests/help.test.tsx tests/beginner-connected-journeys.test.tsx` passed **15/15 tests** on 29 September 2026 (start 12:31:06, duration 20.48 seconds). This consists of 13 public-help/integration checks and the two complete Credit/payroll journeys above. Frontend and test typecheck also passed after unsupported `exact` properties were removed from three role-query options; string role names already use exact matching.

The two journey tests install the domain-backed fake API as Operations, perform permission and preparation actions through rendered UI, then remount with an explicitly different synthetic Finance principal. They neither bypass permissions nor claim to provision a real reviewer. Credit retains the original assessment and separate review after remount; payroll retains the exported manifest and explains that export does not establish payment. No Clerk login, real MFA, external provider, actual employee payment or real two-person acceptance is asserted.

## Acceptance remains separate from automation

No independent first-time participants were observed in this work. Therefore orientation within a minute, 90% unassisted completion, critical distinction comprehension and average ease ≥4/5 are **targets, not measured results**. Do not fill participant results from Playwright or author-led demonstrations. Use the study script and empty measurement template in the evidence package, reporting role, task, assistance, failures and sample size when a study is actually run.
